Here I have some examples of IRL, tangible work I have completed.

First and foremost, my studio needed a vice that could handle the work I regularly do. I needed the vice to occupy a space and not give to an applied force; a "quasi fixed point". Here, I created a clevis from scrap aluminum from Foils recycling center, stainless steel from an old hygenists' chair, and some Mx10 threaded rod. This project's total cost was only <$100 with the vice itself only costing ~$75. After preliminary testing, I would guess that this vice could withstand a load of up to 700 lbf without motion of the vice exceeding more than 1 linear mm.
